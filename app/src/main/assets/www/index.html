About Study Shield

Study Shield was created by Aditya Upadhyay, a young innovator who builds apps to make studying easier.

After creating BruKe AI, a study helper that runs on your own computer, Aditya started Study Shield to solve a problem many students face: distraction.

The idea is simple. Choose the apps that pull your attention away, start a focus session, and let Study Shield protect your study time.

Built with curiosity, one idea at a time.
